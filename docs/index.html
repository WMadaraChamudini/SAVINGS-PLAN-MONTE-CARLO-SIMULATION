Model assumptions and how to use

This client-side simulation models monthly savings accumulation. Investment returns follow a GBM-style log-return process with two market regimes selected using a two-state DTMC. Inflation is also stochastic and follows a GBM-style process.

  • Initial savings: LKR 100,000; monthly contribution: LKR 15,000; target: LKR 5,000,000 in today's purchasing power.
  • Low-return regime: annual drift 3%, volatility 12%.
  • High-return regime: annual drift 9%, volatility 18%.
  • Transition matrix: [[0.90, 0.10], [0.15, 0.85]]. Every path starts in the Low-return regime.
  • Inflation volatility: 2% annually. Investment-return and inflation shocks are independent.
  • Returns are applied before each end-of-month contribution. Final nominal savings are divided by cumulative inflation.
  • Parameters are illustrative assumptions, not forecasts or financial advice.

Adjust the controls and press Start. Pause/Resume temporarily stops or continues the current run. Reset clears the results. Run sensitivity analysis to compare the notebook's contribution and inflation scenarios.
